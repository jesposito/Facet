# Facet

Self-hosted Facet lets one owner reuse their content in pages for different audiences and purposes.

## Language

**Facet**:
A page that presents selected content with its own address, presentation and access settings. It can be shared on its own, included in the owner's site navigation, or used both ways.
_Avoid_: Standalone type, site type, microsite mode

**Standalone page**:
A use of a Facet shared directly for a particular purpose, such as a résumé, proposal or event resource. It can also become part of the site's navigation.

**Site navigation**:
The owner's chosen links between public pages. Inclusion in navigation is independent of a Facet's purpose.

**Access**:
Who may open a Facet: anyone, holders of a share link, people who know its password, or the signed-in owner.
_Avoid_: Purpose, navigation membership

**Share link**:
A link granting access to an unlisted Facet to anyone who holds it, subject to its expiry, use limit and revocation. It isn't tied to a named recipient.
_Avoid_: Recipient-only link, private link

**Section content selection**:
The choice between including all eligible public content and choosing individual items for a section. It describes the section's contents, not the page's purpose or access.

**Item override**:
A change to how a content item appears in one Facet, leaving its source content available to other Facets.
